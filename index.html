        For families and teachers

        • Each activity creates new exercises and can be repeated as many times as needed.
        • Lumi supports, never judges: mistakes are explained and there is always another try.
        • It works on computers, tablets and phones, and can be used with a keyboard.
        • The content follows the Illinois Learning Standards.
        • The portal does not ask for accounts or personal information.

        Languages in Lumi

        In Lumi, the goal is English, and Spanish is a support.

        • From Pre-K to 4th grade, the activities have English and Spanish versions, and a reading series in Spanish helps children keep and grow their home language while they learn to read in English.
        • From 5th grade on, Lumi will be in English. By then, most children who go to school in the United States already read better in English, and the activities have much more text.
        • For children who are still learning English, such as those who have just arrived, an optional Spanish help translates the instructions and the questions, but never the texts or the answers, so the reading practice stays in English.
