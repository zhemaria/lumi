What each play brings

  • 📜 The script

    In English and Spanish, with one color for each character, direct links to every scene and a button to show only one character's lines.

  • 🔊 Hear the lines

    Every line can be read aloud, to rehearse at home or to help children who are still learning to read.

  • 🎭 Character cards

    One card for each part: who the character is, what they are like, which scenes they are in and how many lines they have.

  • 👗 Costumes

    Ideas with simple materials you can find at home or in the classroom.

  • 🏞️ Sets

    What each scene needs, with options for a classroom, a playground or a stage.

  • 🧑‍🏫 Teacher guide

    How to give out the parts, a rehearsal plan and activities for before and after the show.

A part for every child

In Lumi's theater, everyone takes part, each in their own way:

  • Leading parts, with many lines, for children who want a challenge.
  • Parts with a few lines, short and easy to remember.
  • Narrators, who can read from the script on stage.
  • A chorus, who say some lines all together.
  • Parts without lines: sound effects, props, lights and curtain.

Nobody has to learn the lines by heart if they can't: reading the script aloud, with expression, is theater too.

For teachers and families

  • Rehearsing lines aloud, again and again, is good practice for reading fluency.
  • The plays can be performed in English, in Spanish or in both languages, depending on the group.
  • You don't need a stage: a corner of the classroom and a little imagination are enough.
  • Short rehearsals of 15 or 20 minutes work better than one long one.
  • The script can be printed, and the text can be made bigger for anyone who needs it.
