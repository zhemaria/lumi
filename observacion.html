Teacher observation sheet

Lumi asks for no accounts and keeps no grades. Use this sheet to note by hand, while the children work, how each one is doing. Choose the grade, set it up and print it.

Symbols to use

  • ★ Does it without help
  • ✓ Does it with a hint
  • ○ Not yet: will try again

How to use it

  • Watch while the child works and mark with a symbol what you saw most often.
  • The hint a child needed tells you a lot: it shows what to review with that child.
  • “Not yet” is not a bad mark: in Lumi there is always another try.
  • Share it with families in a positive way: what the child can already do and the next step.

Curious Minds is left off the sheet on purpose: those are games to awaken curiosity, creativity and thinking together. They are not tests, so they are not recorded.
