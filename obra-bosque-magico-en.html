El bosque mágico

The Magical Forest · A children's play for the whole school

A play by Vicente Cañón Verdasco

English translation: José M. Fernández, with the author's permission

Three happy villages, some envious witches and wizards, and some fairies and elves who know how to break a curse: by dancing all together. A play for the whole school, from Pre-K to 6th grade.

Cast

These are the author's numbers; every group can grow so the whole school takes part, without changing the text. When a part has a number (Villager 1, Wise Person 3...), that line can be said by one child or by a small group.

Scene 1

Stage direction: (When the curtain opens, we see a set made up of four bushes, with the WITCHES and WIZARDS already hiding behind them. As the NARRATOR comes on stage, the VILLAGERS of the three villages are sitting in front of the stage, facing the audience, in three clearly separate groups that are easy to tell apart by the bright colors of their T-shirts. As the NARRATOR names each village, one by one, its villagers stand up and wave to the audience for a few seconds, then sit back down)

Narrator5th & 6th

Once upon a time, there were three villages that lived near a magical forest in perfect harmony:

Narrator5th & 6th

-The Village of Joy, where they danced all day, every girl and every boy.

Stage direction: (The VILLAGERS of the Village of JOY wave to the audience)

Narrator5th & 6th

-The Village of Friendship, where they always worked hand in hand, the friendliest village in all the land.

Stage direction: (The VILLAGERS of the Village of FRIENDSHIP wave to the audience)

Narrator5th & 6th

And last of all, the Village of Generosity, full of warmth and goodness, as kind as can be.

Stage direction: (The VILLAGERS of the Village of GENEROSITY wave to the audience)

Narrator5th & 6th

Everyone who lived there was cheerful and glad,
and, of course... they lived the happiest life anyone had.
But the witches and wizards who lived in the wood
came to the villages at night, up to no good.

Stage direction: (The WITCHES and WIZARDS pop their heads out from their hiding place)

Narrator5th & 6th

Full of envy, bad energy and spite,
they cast a curse so the villages would be unhappy, day and night.

Stage direction: (The WITCHES and WIZARDS move to the front of the stage and speak defiantly to the audience)

🎵 Music: Track 1 · The New Order · Aaron Kenny (dark cinematic)

Witches3rd & 4th

(Angry) This can't be... these villages are far too happy!

Wizards3rd & 4th

(Angry) Let's cast a spell to make them angry and sad.

Witches3rd & 4th

Yes!

Witches and Wizards3rd & 4th

(Laughing wickedly) Ha, ha, ha...

🎵 Music: Track 1 · The New Order · Aaron Kenny (dark cinematic)

Stage direction: (They start to brew the spell. One WITCH and one WIZARD bring a big pot or cauldron on stage, and everyone mimes dropping things into it)

Witches and Wizards3rd & 4th

Frog drool, slug eyes and... city smog,
let happiness vanish... into the fog!

Wizards3rd & 4th

There! It's done!

Witches3rd & 4th

The spell is ready! (Laughing) Ha, ha, ha...

Witches and Wizards3rd & 4th

Let's go spread it... now!

🎵 Music: Track 1 · The New Order · Aaron Kenny (dark cinematic)

Stage direction: (The same WITCH and WIZARD carry the pot offstage. When they come back, they all form a little “choo-choo train” and go hide, in several groups, behind the bushes of the forest)

Scene 2
Narrator5th & 6th

The witches and wizards waited, hidden away, for the Village of Joy to come to the forest, so they could cast their curse on them that very same day.

(Village of Joy)

Stage direction: (To the music of track 2, the VILLAGERS of the Village of JOY come on stage and dance a routine that shows joy)

Stage direction: (When the music ends, the WITCHES and WIZARDS come out of hiding and cast their curse on the VILLAGERS of the Village of Joy)

Witches and Wizards3rd & 4th

Zip, zap... zip, zap... joy will disappear!

Stage direction: (The WITCHES and WIZARDS quickly hide again)

Stage direction: (The VILLAGERS of the Village of JOY look at one another, very sad)

Villagers 1 and 2Pre-K & K

What happened to us?

Villagers 3 and 4Pre-K & K

We're so sad!

All the villagersPre-K & K

(Very sad) Oh! Oh!

Stage direction: (To some slightly sad music, the VILLAGERS of the Village of JOY leave the stage very slowly and go back to where they started. They all walk looking down at the floor, very sad)

🎵 Music: Track 3 · St. Francis · Josh Lippi & The Overtimers (rock, sad)

Scene 5
Narrator5th & 6th

The Villages of Joy, Friendship and Generosity were sad and full of despair, because they had lost their happiness, which was, for them, their most precious treasure. To find a solution, the wisest people met in the forest that day, and now you will see what they had to say.

🎵 Music: Track 6 · Sleeping Sheep · The Green Orbs (children's, calm)

Stage direction: (The WISE PEOPLE come on stage and walk around, worried, looking for a solution to their problem, which is not easy to find. In despair, they gather in the center of the stage)

Wise People 1 and 23rd & 4th

What happened to us?

Wise People 3 and 43rd & 4th

How can we bring happiness back to our villages?

Stage direction: (The WISE PEOPLE shrug their shoulders to show that they cannot find any solution to their big problem)

Scene 6
Narrator5th & 6th

Then suddenly, from the magical forest, some fairies and elves came near; they hurried up to the wise people of each village and said, for all to hear...

🎵 Music: Track 7 · Kinderszenen, Op. 15, No. 1: “Of Foreign Lands and Peoples” · Robert Schumann (piano)

Stage direction: (To cheerful music, a group of FAIRIES comes in from one side, circles around the WISE PEOPLE and stands on one side of the stage. A little later, a group of ELVES comes in from the other side, also circles around the WISE PEOPLE and stands on the side opposite the FAIRIES)

We have come to help you.

Wise People 1 and 23rd & 4th

(Very surprised) Oh, fairies!

Wise People 3 and 43rd & 4th

(Very surprised) Oh, elves!

All the Wise People3rd & 4th

Wooooow!!!

Fairies1st & 2nd

You are under the spell of some wicked witches and wizards.

All the Wise People3rd & 4th

So what can we do?

Elves1st & 2nd

Tonight we will go to the cave where the witches and wizards live...

Fairies1st & 2nd

And while they're asleep, we'll all dance as one.

Elves and Fairies1st & 2nd

And the curse will be undone!

Wise People 1 and 23rd & 4th

Thank you so much!

Wise People 3 and 43rd & 4th

That's just what we'll do!

Stage direction: (The FAIRIES and the ELVES leave the stage, each group the way it came in, always to the same music that played when they entered)

🎵 Music: Track 7 · Kinderszenen, Op. 15, No. 1: “Of Foreign Lands and Peoples” · Robert Schumann (piano)

Narrator5th & 6th

When the wise people of each village heard the fairies and the elves, they were filled with hope, you see, and they ran to tell their neighbors everything, as fast as can be.

Stage direction: (The VILLAGERS stand up, with their backs to the audience, to listen to their wise people, who have moved to the front of the stage to speak to them)

Scene 7
Wise People 1 and 23rd & 4th

Listen, listen! Some fairies and elves are coming to help us!

All the villagersPre-K & K

Fantastic!

Wise People 3 and 43rd & 4th

All the villagersPre-K & K

All the Wise People3rd & 4th

Stage direction: (The VILLAGERS crouch down and keep quiet. The WISE PEOPLE of each village leave the stage and split into two groups: the first group goes with the FAIRIES, and the second, with the ELVES)

Narrator5th & 6th

That night the villagers climbed up to the cave, as quiet as could be, while the witches and the wizards slept so peacefully. Quiet! Because deep inside the forest, eager to break the spell, the fairies and the elves were waiting for them as well.

🎵 Music: Track 8 · Lullaby (Wiegenlied) · Johannes Brahms (lullaby)

Stage direction: (The WITCHES and WIZARDS come on stage and lie down to sleep in the center. A little later, the FAIRIES and ELVES tiptoe in and give a signal for the WISE PEOPLE to come in, together with all the VILLAGERS of the three villages, who have also come up onto the stage without making a sound. Together, they all form a big circle around the WITCHES and WIZARDS, who are still asleep)

Scene 8

(Asking for silence) Shhhh! Don't make a sound.

Fairies1st & 2nd

On the count of three, we'll start to dance.

Fairies and Elves1st & 2nd

Yes!!!

🎵 Music: Track 9 · Twirly Tops · The Green Orbs (children's, happy)

Stage direction: (When the music ends, all the VILLAGERS, the WISE PEOPLE, the FAIRIES and the ELVES spread out toward the sides of the stage, turning the circle into a half circle, so the audience can see the WITCHES and WIZARDS, who are starting to wake up)

Witches3rd & 4th

Narrator5th & 6th

And that is how, with all the positive energy the three villages gathered together, and with the help of the fairies and the elves, they defeated the negativity and the wickedness of those rude, boastful witches and wizards.
And they all lived happily ever after.
And snip, snap, snout... our play is all played out.

🎵 Music: Track 10 · Dancing on Green Grass · The Green Orbs (children's, upbeat)
